# Source provenance and reuse terms

This collection curates adaptable design guides and independent qualification runners for Tendriller and Chronolog. The generated [source map](distribution/source-map.json) records the exact historical commit URLs cited in cookbook frontmatter (`sources:`). Historical links are provenance evidence, not supported legacy APIs.

## What is retained

Prose captures design decisions, tradeoffs, algorithm semantics, and failure scenarios. Small snippets and qualification checks become application-owned source when copied and adapted; they are not a component library or dependency graph.

## Terms

Existing source attribution and third-party notices remain unchanged; this repository grants no additional license beyond existing notices. Evidence labels describe reviewed prose or exercised artifact checks, not universal guarantees.
