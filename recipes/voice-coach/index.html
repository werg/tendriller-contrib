Hands-free commands

Open the standard Tendriller control, choose Settings and history → Hands-free wake phrase, and set the phrase to Hey coach.

Select your recognition and privacy mode, then tap Start hands-free in the Talk tab. Say “Hey coach, what's next?” or say the wake phrase and then speak your command.

Tap Stop in the microphone indicator whenever you want to turn listening off.

Hands-free recognition requires a supported browser and an open, unlocked page. “Keep screen on” can help during an activity but uses battery. You can always use the built-in conversation microphone or type.
