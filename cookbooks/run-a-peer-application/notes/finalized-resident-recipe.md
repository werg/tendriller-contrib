# Historical resident recipe evidence

The resident/chronological composition has been retired. Current applications use
[one public peer session](../COOKBOOK.md), and the [current gates](../checks/README.md)
exercise its native, browser, SQL, RTC and hosted-participant adapters. Optional
stronger guarantees extend that session's declared capabilities; they do not
select a second application API, journal or replay stack.

The complete [earlier recipe](https://github.com/werg/tendriller-contrib/blob/11c445e4d25e7b8ca415f3327c8049fcadc9f547/cookbooks/run-a-peer-application/notes/finalized-resident-recipe.md),
[page/transport specimens](https://github.com/werg/tendriller-contrib/tree/11c445e4d25e7b8ca415f3327c8049fcadc9f547/cookbooks/run-a-peer-application/checks),
and [resident connection snippets](https://github.com/werg/tendriller-contrib/tree/11c445e4d25e7b8ca415f3327c8049fcadc9f547/cookbooks/run-a-peer-application/snippets)
remain available at their recorded source snapshot. They are not maintained as
compatibility code in the current tree.

Earlier evidence remains unchanged, including:

- [Compact membership and resident qualification](compact-membership-resident-qualification-2026-11-17.md), with its exact source and archive identities.
- [Recorded RTC qualification](rtc-final-qualification-2026-11-17.md), with its observed route and runtime scope.

Those records describe the former guarantees and execution order. They do not
qualify the current session's generation recovery, participant policies or signed

prerelease implementation.
